1. PROMPT TẠO LẠI CÂU CHUYỆN :
Transform the following story into a highly cinematic, emotionally intense English narrative that readers won’t be able to stop reading, while preserving the original plot, emotional beats, character relationships, and key story details completely intact.

The rewrite should feel immersive, human, suspenseful, and emotionally authentic — like a powerful scene from a film rather than exaggerated social media bait.

Follow these rules strictly:

1. OPENING HOOK (First 2–3 sentences):
Start with the most emotionally charged, tense, mysterious, or devastating moment from the story.

The opening MUST:

* instantly create emotional pressure, urgency, or discomfort
* contain a strong curiosity gap
* imply that something is already wrong
* hint at a shocking truth, betrayal, danger, accusation, loss, or hidden conflict

Do NOT:

* start with character names
* use generic setup or background
* use overused viral phrases like “you won’t believe,” “don’t scroll,” or “watch until the end”

Lead with emotion, tension, silence, fear, conflict, or impossible situations.

2. TONE & STYLE:
Write in a warm, cinematic, emotionally grounded tone.

The story should feel:

* realistic
* visual
* deeply human
* emotionally immersive

Use:

* short punchy sentences during tense moments
* longer flowing sentences during emotional reflection
* vivid but natural wording
* subtle emotional detail and micro-reactions

Avoid:

* robotic pacing
* repetitive AI storytelling patterns
* exaggerated melodrama
* recycled TikTok/Facebook phrasing

The story should read like a Netflix drama or emotional cinematic film scene.

3. STRUCTURE:
Use short mobile-friendly paragraphs (2–4 sentences max).

Every paragraph should:

* increase emotional tension
* reveal new emotional information
* deepen curiosity naturally
* escalate suspense gradually
* pull the reader deeper emotionally

4. STORY FLOW & CONSISTENCY:
Preserve ALL important:

* plot points
* emotional turns
* character dynamics
* relationships
* story logic

Do NOT:

* invent fake twists
* create misleading drama
* add random fictional elements

You may change character names naturally to match gender and tone.

5. EMOTIONAL ESCALATION:
Continuously build emotional pressure throughout the story.

Prioritize:

* betrayal
* hidden truths
* fear of what happens next
* emotional conflict
* trust breaking apart
* moral tension
* visual scene-building
* unresolved danger

Add subtle:

* internal thoughts
* pauses
* facial reactions
* body language
* silence
* emotional contrast

The reader should constantly feel that something life-changing is about to happen.

6. CLIMAX RULE (VERY IMPORTANT):
Stop the story at the single most emotionally explosive unresolved moment — right before the truth is revealed, the confrontation happens, or the final decision is made.

Do NOT:
* resolve the conflict
* explain the aftermath
* summarize what happens next

The ending should create overwhelming natural curiosity.

7. ORIGINALITY & HUMAN FEEL:
Rewrite all narration and dialogue naturally using fresh phrasing and varied sentence structure.

Avoid:

* repetitive hooks
* recycled viral wording
* obvious AI patterns
* forced engagement bait
* overdramatic social media phrasing

The final result should feel like an original cinematic retelling written by a human storyteller.

8. LENGTH:
Keep the rewritten story between 550–650 words.

9. FINAL LINE:
End with a subtle continuation-style line that naturally implies the story continues.

Examples of tone:

* “Nobody in that room was prepared for what came next.”
* “That was the moment everything finally began to unravel.”
* “The truth stayed buried for years after that night.”
* “What happened next changed every life in that house.”
And the final line naturally with a subtle 👇 emoji.

10. FORMAT:
Present the final result in clean, well-formatted paragraphs optimized for Facebook/Reels/social media reading.
